# Why these records do not need an application front end

A service manager's weekly work is reviewing exceptions, assigning next steps, recording outcomes and preparing account documents. These jobs work from a private database and a coding agent. The CLI returns facts in text or JSON; branded HTML snapshots make them readable at a review meeting.

The snapshots do not provide mobile capture, offline work, live collaboration, drag and drop, a customer portal or authentication screens. They are generated files, not a hosted application. Meter telemetry and supply control belong to their operational systems. A coding agent can change files and records, so restrict its database and filesystem access to authorised operators.

Enterprise DNA can build a team screen, portal or connection as scoped custom work. The free base is commercial utility account administration, not feature parity with the full Salesforce industry platform.
